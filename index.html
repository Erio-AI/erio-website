Home service businesses are growing faster with AI than ever before.

[ The Shift ]

In 2020, only ~5% of home service contractors were using any kind of AI. Calls went to voicemail, leads slipped through cracks, and quotes were written by hand.

By 2022, adoption climbed to ~18% as basic AI tools — chat, lead routing, smart scheduling — started showing up inside the major contractor CRMs.

In 2024, more than half of contractors (~52%) had adopted AI in some form, with AI receptionists capturing millions of after-hours calls competitors used to lose.

Today, roughly 78% of contractors rely on AI to book jobs, answer calls, and grow — and the ones using Erio are doing it at the highest level.
